Built for your whole Mac,

⌃⌥Space

  • Your whole Mac

    Apps, windows, screens, the web. Say how you want your desk and it sets it up.

    Code on my big screen, Chrome and Terminal on the laptop.

  • Knows your files

    Finds any file by what's inside, not just its name.

    Find my notes about the Tokyo trip.

  • Sees your screen

    Say “this” and it looks. It reads the error and fixes the line.

    What's wrong with this code?

  • Writes where you are

    The words land in the box you're already in, in your voice.

    Reply to this. Thursday works, send me a time.

  • Works in the background

    Big jobs run in their own browser window, behind your apps. Your pointer stays yours.

    Find me a desk lamp under thirty dollars on Amazon.

  • Shows you where to click

    Ask how, and it points at the spot on your screen.

    Where do I turn on Night Shift?

  • Answers on a call

    Meeting Copilot listens along and answers what you missed.

    Sorry, what budget did we just agree on?

  • Remembers what you teach it

    Teach it once. Next time one word is enough.

    When I say focus mode, close Slack and open my to-do.

Hold ⌃ ⌥ Space and say it

Talk while you hold the keys. Let go and it happens. Or skip the voice and type.

Point at what you mean

Circle it with your mouse while you talk. Atticus looks there and explains, step by step, on your screen.
